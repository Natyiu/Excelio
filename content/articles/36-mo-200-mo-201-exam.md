# MO-200 and MO-201 Excel Exams: Skills Covered and How to Prepare

The Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) certifications are among the best-known ways to prove Excel skills on a CV. There are two Excel levels: **MO-200 (Associate)** and **MO-201 (Expert)**. Here's what each one covers and how to prepare efficiently.

## MO-200 vs MO-201 at a glance

| | MO-200 (Associate) | MO-201 (Expert) |
|---|---|---|
| Level | Core, everyday Excel | Advanced analysis and automation |
| Who it's for | Students, admin, entry-level analysts | Analysts, finance, power users |
| Typical focus | Formatting, tables, basic formulas, charts | Advanced formulas, macros, what-if analysis, PivotTables |
| Prerequisite | None | None (but Associate-level skills assumed) |

Both are **performance-based**: you complete tasks in a live Excel environment rather than answering trivia. Expect a project-based format with around 50 minutes to complete it. Check the current timing when you book.

## MO-200 (Associate) skill areas

Based on Microsoft's published outline, the main areas are:

1. **Manage worksheets and workbooks**: import data from text/CSV, navigate with Go To and named ranges, page setup, freeze panes, print settings, inspect the workbook.
2. **Manage data cells and ranges**: Paste Special, AutoFill, insert/delete cells, merge, number formats, Format Painter, named ranges, sparklines, conditional formatting.
3. **Manage tables and table data**: create and format tables, add total rows, sort and filter.
4. **Perform operations by using formulas and functions**: relative/absolute/mixed references, structured references, SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA, COUNTBLANK, IF, SORT, UNIQUE, text functions (LEFT, RIGHT, MID, UPPER, LOWER, PROPER, LEN, CONCAT, TEXTJOIN).
5. **Manage charts**: create, modify, format, add alt text.

## MO-201 (Expert) skill areas

1. **Manage workbook options and settings**: templates, version history, protection, macros (enable, record simple macros, copy between workbooks), formula calculation options.
2. **Manage and format data**: Flash Fill, advanced fill series, custom number formats, data validation, advanced conditional formatting rules, removing duplicates.
3. **Create advanced formulas and macros**: nested functions with AND/OR/NOT, IFS, SWITCH, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, COUNTIFS, MAXIFS, MINIFS, XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, MATCH, INDEX, date functions (NOW, TODAY, WEEKDAY, WORKDAY), what-if tools (Goal Seek, Scenario Manager), forecasting, PMT, formula auditing, LET, FILTER, SORTBY.
4. **Manage advanced charts and tables**: combo and advanced charts, PivotTables and PivotCharts, slicers, calculated fields, grouping.

## A 4-week study plan (MO-200)

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Workbook navigation, page setup, formatting, Paste Special, named ranges |
| 2 | Tables, sorting, filtering, conditional formatting |
| 3 | Formulas and references, text functions, SORT and UNIQUE |
| 4 | Charts, then two full timed practice runs |

For **MO-201**, add two weeks for advanced formulas, macros and what-if analysis.

## Tips from people who pass

- **Practise in the ribbon, not just with formulas.** Many tasks are "apply this setting", so know where every command lives.
- **Read the task literally.** If it says "named range *Sales*", the grader checks for exactly that name.
- **Don't leave a task half-done.** Partial work generally doesn't score. Move on and come back.
- **Watch the clock.** Speed is the main thing that separates passing and failing, and it comes from repetition.
- **Use the official practice materials** (GMetrix is the practice test commonly bundled with Certiport vouchers) to get used to the interface.

---

## Is it worth it?

A MOS certificate is a clear signal on a CV for admin, finance and analyst roles, and some universities and employers fund the exam. For senior roles, a portfolio of real work (a dashboard, a model) often carries more weight. Ideally, have both.
